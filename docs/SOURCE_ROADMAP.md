# Mobility source roadmap

1. Export MiT/MiD 2023 exposure by mode.
2. Extract Destatis/CARE endpoints by matching mode, year and population.
3. Join only compatible exposure-event pairs.
4. Estimate uncertainty from counts and survey weights.
5. Validate against held-out years and regions.
6. Promote parameter status only after Evidence Gate and calibration.

Priority: car, motorcycle/moped, bicycle/pedelec, walking, bus/coach, rail, tram/subway, commercial aviation, ferry. Niche modes remain separate and blocked until exposure-compatible evidence exists.
