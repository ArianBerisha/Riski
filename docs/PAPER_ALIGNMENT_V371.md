# P-QRA alignment

Structurally retained: additive hazard, risk-specific barriers, P = 1 - exp(-H), daily aggregation and confidence tiers. Numerically changed: the original worked car example used about 0.0031 µMort/km, belt effectiveness around 45-50%, ESP around 20-25%, and night-only driving around 15-20%. The app uses newer public-data calibration and is therefore not numerically identical. Not yet generalized: R(t)*a*exp(bt) and the full block-diagonal barrier incidence model across all risks.
